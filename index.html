// What we do

Aureven makes video easier to understand.

We build AI that helps people process video faster and more intelligently. Instead of manually scrubbing through long footage, Aureven identifies important moments, understands visual context, and produces summaries people can use right away.

Why it matters

Video carries far more than spoken words — movement, objects, framing, and context all shape what actually happened. Aureven is built to read all of it, not just the audio track.
